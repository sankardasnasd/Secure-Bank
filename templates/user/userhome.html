Four protections working in the background

Each one runs as its own service, so your banking stays fast even when one is busy.

Phishing link check

A trained model rates each link as safe or phishing before you tap it.

App file scan

APK files are marked clean, suspicious or malicious before install.

Traffic watch

A sandbox spots request floods and blocks the sender automatically.

Transfer alerts

Every transfer gets a risk score, and flagged ones are sent to you by email.

What happens when you check something

The detection services have no screen of their own. You see every result here.

  1. You submit

    A link, an APK file or a transfer.

  2. Gateway routes

    It goes to the right service.

  3. Service decides

    The model or rules give a verdict.

  4. You are told

    See it on your dashboard and by email.
